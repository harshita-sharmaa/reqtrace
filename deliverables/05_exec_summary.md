# 5. Executive summary: purchasing before the ERP rollout

**To:** VP Finance & Administration · **From:** Business Analyst · **Re:** Purchasing controls (Jul–Sep 2026)

**Bottom line:** Today's email-and-Excel purchasing process is losing money and bypassing policy. We likely overpaid one vendor **~$66K**, and **$111K** of spend skipped the required Finance Director approval. The ERP fixes this only if we configure the controls below. Moving the current process onto the new system as-is won't fix it.

## What we found (63 requests, $191K approved)
1. **Manual re-typing caused payment errors.** 3 POs were logged at the wrong amount; one was **paid at 10x** ($73K vs. $7.3K approved) and another $87K error is still unpaid.
2. **The $5K approval rule isn't enforced.** 12 of 16 requests over $5K ($111K) were approved by department managers alone.
3. **Requests go missing.** 7 approved requests never got a PO, and 3 were resubmitted because requesters couldn't see their status. 32% of decisions take more than 5 days.

## What we recommend
1. **Now:** Recover the REQ-023 overpayment and hold REQ-057.
2. **ERP configuration (Must-haves):** amount-based routing to the Finance Director, auto-created POs with no re-typing, a required GL code, and a 3-way match before payment.
3. **ERP configuration (Should-haves):** a requester status view, duplicate warnings, and a 3/5-day approval SLA.

## Decisions needed from you
- Confirm the $5,000 threshold and whether it applies per request or per vendor per month.
- Name a backup approver for each department for SLA escalations.
